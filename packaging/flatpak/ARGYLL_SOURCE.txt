ArgyllCMS in the freeglaz Flatpak
=================================

The programs in this folder (bin/), the reference data (ref/) and the udev rules
(usb/55-Argyll.rules) are copied unmodified from the official ArgyllCMS 3.5.0
binary archive for Linux on x86_64:

  https://www.argyllcms.com/Argyll_V3.5.0_linux_x86_64_bin.tgz
  sha256 0d1da052de22396f4141c5ca3ee0c17a434bc142048a327dd86565a9f20d0c13

ArgyllCMS is Copyright Graeme W. Gill and distributed under the GNU Affero
General Public License version 3 (see License.txt in this folder). The
programs it is built from (some files are under other licences compatible
with it) are described in that archive's documentation.

Corresponding source
--------------------
The source of these programs is the official ArgyllCMS 3.5.0 source archive:

  https://www.argyllcms.com/Argyll_V3.5.0_src.zip
  sha256 f8576ce5589fd15620abb73ff049ea31f55ddbd1bba6d1ffa87452658e7bc85f

An unmodified copy of that archive ships in this package, in the
Corresponding Source archive of the chartread-fgK measurement engine:

  /app/chartread-fgK/source/chartread-fgK-3.5.0-fg3.1-src.tar.gz
    -> upstream/Argyll_V3.5.0_src.zip

It is also attached to every freeglaz release on GitHub.
